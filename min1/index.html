Chapter I — About us

still. is a small wellness company making simple, natural essentials for everyday calm, rest and care.

“Wellness shouldn’t be complicated.”

Our promise. Natural ingredients, listed in full on the front of every pack. Independent lab testing on every batch. Free returns within 30 days.
